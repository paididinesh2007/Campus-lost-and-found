Safety Protocol

Tips for a Safe Handover on Campus

  • Always meet in open public spots like the Central Library foyer or Security Control Desk.
  • For electronic devices, ask the owner to unlock the passcode or verify their unique wallpaper/serial number.
  • For student IDs and bank cards, confirm their student enrollment number before handing it over.

Posting Guidelines

Follow these simple tips to get your item recovered quickly:

  • 1
    Be Specific

    Mention color, brand, stickers, scratches, or unique details.

  • 2
    Pinpoint Spot

    Instead of just "Library", note "2nd Floor quiet study room, Table 4".

  • 3
    Add a Clear Photo

    Visual previews speed up recovery by over 70% on campus.

  • 4
    Protect Sensitive Info

    Don't publish full debit card numbers or passwords publicly.
